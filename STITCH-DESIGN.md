Reference: Creative Director Editorial Portfolio, project 2431448683515779180.

- Home: black canvas, bold three-line masthead, grey secondary words, red punctuation, alternating image/text selected-work rows.
- Design: warm paper, catalogue rules and red indices, full-width opening work, asymmetric two-column archive and dark feature panel.
- Design detail: bold title, specification panel, full-proportion gallery with red progress indicator, dark next-project transition.
- Video: white navigation above a black moving-image archive, panoramic first cover, paired secondary films.
- Video detail: dark specification panel, framed click-to-load player and process notes.
- About: oversized practice statement, paper dossier, modular capability panels.
- Contact: black inquiry page, oversized headline, precise field labels and underlined inputs.

The existing Foldcraft identity, real email, routes, filters, complete-artwork carousel, responsive image delivery and email-draft behavior remain the application data and functionality. Reference portraits, client names, awards and claimed credits are not attributed to the user. Example artwork and missing personal content remain labelled.

Phone layouts collapse editorial rows into single-column cards; long titles wrap. Reduced-motion and keyboard interaction remain supported.
